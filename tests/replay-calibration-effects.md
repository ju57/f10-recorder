# Replay calibration launch effects and plan bounds

This page owns detailed calibration/continuation effects formerly listed in
[test-effects.md](test-effects.md). It preserves the declared routes and their
plan-specific cohorts, including failed/replacement attempts. These are not
interchangeable default budgets or new execution authorization. A route runs only
within its applicable reviewed plan and current-task authorization.

Use [storage admission](../docs/storage-admission.md) for the capacity-policy
candidate and duration-specific calibration rules, and
[experiment admission](experiment-contract.md#before-new-recordings) to assess
retained evidence before new collection. Explicit historical 500 ms observation
contracts below do not qualify the candidate's 2,000 ms coverage horizon.
The prospective/replacement bounds retain their original failed-attempt charges;
choose the exact applicable plan rather than combining numbers from different
cohorts. Current completion state belongs to the owning issue.
